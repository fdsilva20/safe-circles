# Listening guidelines for facilitators

You are not a counsellor and you are not expected to be one. Your job is to hold a safe space where people listen to each other. These guidelines are how you do that.

## Before the session
- Arrive early. Arrange chairs in a circle with no table in the middle.
- Have the group guidelines, the topic and the referral directory with you, on paper or on your phone (the toolkit works offline once opened).
- Know the room: exits, a quiet corner for a private conversation, water.

## Opening (10 minutes)
- Welcome everyone by display name. Read the group guidelines aloud.
- Run one icebreaker so every voice is heard.
- Introduce the topic and the opening question.

## While people share
- **Listen with your whole attention.** Face the speaker. Put your phone away.
- **Do not fix, advise or compare.** Replace "you should" with "thank you for sharing that".
- **Reflect, do not interpret.** "It sounds like that was very hard" rather than "you must feel angry".
- **Allow silence.** Count to ten in your head before filling a pause.
- **Protect the pass.** If someone passes, move on warmly. Return to them only if they signal.
- **Hold the time.** Gently invite others if one person has spoken for long. "Thank you. Let us hear from someone else."
- **Name disrespect at once, and gently.** "Let us come back to our guideline about listening without judging."

## When someone becomes distressed
- Stay calm and slow down. Offer water and a moment.
- Ask what they need: to continue, to pause, or to step out with you or a trusted person.
- Do not ask for details of what happened to them.
- After the session, check in privately. Share the referral directory if it fits.
- If what they share raises a safeguarding concern, follow the safeguarding protocol.

## Closing (10 minutes)
- Run the closing round. Everyone speaks once, briefly.
- Remind the group of confidentiality and the next session.
- Point to the referral directory for anyone who wants more support.
- Record only the attendance count on the platform. Never names, never what was said.

## After the session
- Take ten minutes for yourself. Facilitation is tiring.
- Note anything that needs the safeguarding protocol and act on it the same day.
- If you need support yourself, contact Red Dot Foundation's facilitator support line.
