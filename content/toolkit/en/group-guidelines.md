# Group guidelines

Every Safe Circle follows the same guidelines. The facilitator reads them aloud at the start of each session, and anyone may ask for them to be read again.

1. **What is said here stays here.** We do not repeat what others share, in person or online. We may talk about our own experience of the circle, never anyone else's.
2. **We listen without fixing.** Nobody is here to give advice, diagnose or rescue. We listen, and we thank people for sharing.
3. **We speak from our own experience.** We use "I" rather than "you should". We do not speak for others.
4. **Everyone may pass.** Nobody has to speak. Silence is welcome.
5. **One voice at a time.** We do not interrupt, and we do not have side conversations.
6. **We respect difference.** Of religion, caste, gender, sexuality, language, ability and belief. Disrespect is named gently and stopped.
7. **No photos, no recordings, no notes about others.** Phones stay away during the circle.
8. **We take care of ourselves.** Anyone may step out and return. The facilitator will check in with you.
9. **Safety comes first.** If someone is in danger, the facilitator will follow the safeguarding protocol, which may mean seeking help outside the circle. The facilitator will tell you if that is happening.
10. **We arrive on time and we end on time.**

Safe Circles is for adults aged 18 and above. If something in a circle worries you, you can report a concern on this platform, anonymously if you prefer.
