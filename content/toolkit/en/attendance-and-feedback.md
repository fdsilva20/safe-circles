# Attendance and feedback

## Attendance
After each session, open your facilitator dashboard and record **how many people came**. That is all the platform stores about attendance: a number.

- Do not keep a paper list of names. If you must note who was present for safety reasons (for example an evacuation), destroy it after the session.
- A session with zero attendance is still worth recording; it tells us where the schedule or location is not working.
- If you cancel a session, cancel it on the platform so registered participants are not left waiting.

## Participant feedback
Participants are invited to give anonymous feedback after each session: did they feel safe, did they feel heard, would they come again, plus an optional comment.

- Feedback is anonymous by construction. The platform records that a person gave feedback, so it cannot be given twice, but never links the answers to the person.
- You will see your circle's feedback only once at least five responses have been given, so no single person's answer can be identified.
- Read comments as a gift, not a verdict. Bring anything puzzling to your facilitator peer group.

## What the network publishes
The open data page shows live, anonymised totals for the whole network: active circles, sessions held, attendance, facilitators trained, and aggregated feedback. Small counts are suppressed. Nothing identifies a circle's participants.
