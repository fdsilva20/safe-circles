# Icebreakers

Five to ten minutes at the start of each session. Choose one. The aim is for every voice to be heard once before the topic begins.

1. **Weather report.** "If your week were weather, what would it be?" One sentence each.
2. **Object from your bag.** Everyone takes out one object and says why it matters.
3. **Two truths and a hope.** Two true things about your week and one thing you hope for next week.
4. **Rose, thorn, bud.** One good thing, one hard thing, one thing you are looking forward to.
5. **Name and a sound.** Say your name with a movement or sound; the group repeats it. Good for a new group.
6. **One song.** A song that matched your mood this week, and why.
7. **Gratitude circle.** One thing you are grateful for that costs nothing.
8. **Word of the week.** One word that sums up your week, no explanation needed.
9. **Where I come from.** One line beginning "I come from..." about a place, a person or a habit.
10. **The chair.** Each person says one thing they are leaving outside the door for the next ninety minutes.

Facilitator tip: go first, keep it short, and thank each person by their display name.
