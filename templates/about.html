Safe Circles is a decentralised, community-led peer-support model: confidential, non-judgemental listening circles for mental wellbeing, gender equality and violence prevention, run by trained community volunteers. It grew out of Red Dot Foundation's in-person circles in Satara, Pune and Mumbai.

This platform helps people find a circle, attend one, or train to lead one, and gives facilitators a toolkit: weekly topics, icebreakers, arts-based activities, listening guidelines, a safeguarding protocol and referral directories.

Who it is for

Adults aged 18 and above. Circles for adolescents are run offline in schools by trained adult facilitators using the same toolkit; no young person has an account here and no data about them is stored.

Privacy by design

  • You sign in with an email link. No password, no phone number, no legal name.
  • Other participants see only the display name you choose.
  • Exact venues are shared only with people who have registered.
  • Nothing said inside a circle is ever recorded here. Facilitators record only how many people came.
  • Feedback is anonymous by construction and shown only in aggregate above a minimum number of responses.

Where AI is used, and where it is not

AI supports facilitators only: it can draft weekly topics for Red Dot Foundation to review before they are published, and it can answer a facilitator's question about handling a situation, grounded in the safeguarding protocol and referral directory. It never speaks to participants and never sees anything said in a circle.

Open source and open data

The code is open source under the Apache 2.0 licence so other communities and organisations can run their own network. Live anonymised figures are published on the open data page and at /data.json.

A Murmur AI and Red Dot Foundation project.
